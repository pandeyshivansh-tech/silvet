---
name: Styling principles
description: Product constraints for recommendations and future AI styling work.
---

Indian fashion is a first-class part of SILVET recommendations, not a special-case fallback. Explanations must be grounded in actual garment metadata, never invented colour, style, occasion or fabric attributes.

**Why:** The user explicitly required equal standing for Indian, Western and fusion clothing and factual explanations.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints when extending compatibility policy, ranking or explanations.

A future AI styling layer must build on the pure recommendation engine and existing domain architecture, not replace them.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested an extensible engine for future AI styling without replacing the domain architecture.

**How to apply:** Keep domain validation authoritative and AI integration outside the pure engine.
