---
name: Responsive brand identity
description: User-required responsive logo policy that supersedes the older below-360px compact rule.
---

Phones and compact screens must show only a bespoke SVG Selvedge S, not a typeset character, emoji or generic icon. Desktop shows the complete SILVET wordmark. Tablet uses the full wordmark wherever available space allows, otherwise the compact symbol.

The mark and wordmark must form a coherent identity, shared across landing, authentication, app navigation, loading states, favicon and browser metadata. Explore the Selvedge concept from page 1 of the approved PDF; do not introduce an arbitrary font or generic logo. Preserve its colour rules and legibility at small sizes on both Warm Chalk and Midnight Aubergine.

**Why:** The user explicitly required this responsive identity across SILVET, replacing the older documentation's compact-logo threshold.

**How to apply:** Use the approved phone/tablet/desktop ranges when planning responsive branding, and available container space for tablet variants. Obtain approval before implementing logo assets or application changes; the identity requirement is not approval of any final symbol drawing.
